# KLG Citation Rules Quick Reference

## Citation Placement

Citations go in separate citation sentences, not embedded in
substantive sentences.

**Correct:**
The injunction is mandatory in nature. (Mills v. People (2025)
98 Cal.App.3d 861, 865.)

**Incorrect:**
The injunction is mandatory in nature under Mills v. People
(2025) 98 Cal.App.3d 861, 865.

## California Cases (California Style Manual)

### Full Citation
Party v. Party (Year) Volume Reporter Page, Pincite.

Examples:
- (Automotriz Del Golfo De California v. Resnick (1957) 47 Cal.2d 792, 796.)
- (People v. Pacific Landmark, LLC (2005) 129 Cal.App.4th 1203, 1212.)
- (Mills v. People (2025) 98 Cal.App.3d 861, 865.)

### Short Form (after first full citation)
(Party, supra, Volume Reporter at p. Page.)

Examples:
- (Automotriz, supra, 47 Cal.2d at p. 796.)
- (Mills, supra, 98 Cal.App.3d at p. 865.)

### Rules
- Italicize case name only (not reporter, volume, page)
- No "at" before pincite in full citations
- Use "at p." or "at pp." in short-form citations
- No superscript in reporter abbreviations
- Year in parentheses after case name
- Pincite after full citation page with comma separator

## Federal Cases (Bluebook)

### Full Citation
Party v. Party (Year) Volume Reporter Page, Pincite.

Note: KLG uses the California Style Manual parenthetical date
format even for federal cases (date after party names, not at end).

Examples:
- (Lucas v. South Carolina Coastal Council (1992) 505 U.S. 1003, 1015-1019.)
- (Timbs v. Indiana (2019) 586 U.S. 146, 149-150.)
- (Central Hudson Gas & Elec. Corp. v. Public Serv. Comm'n (1980) 447 U.S. 557, 566.)

## Record Citations

### Appendix Format
(Volume-AA-Page.) — no spaces in "1AA"

Examples:
- (1-AA-1.)
- (19-AA-3878-3883.)

### Document-Name Format
(Date Document Name, PDF p. Page.)

Examples:
- (2024-11-13 Statement of Decision, PDF p. 18.)
- (2025-02-20 Preliminary Injunction, PDF pp. 1-4.)
- (2026-01-27 Judgment, PDF p. 4.)
- (2023-12-22 Second Amended Complaint, PDF [¶ 25].)

### Declaration Format
(Date Declarant Decl. at Page.)

Examples:
- (2026-02-03 Spencer Hoopes Decl. at 3-4.)
- (2026-02-03 Lindsay Hoopes Decl. at 2-3.)

### Reporter's Transcript
(RT Page:Line.) or (Vol. X RT Page:Line.)
(TT Page:Line-Page:Line.) for trial transcript

### Exhibit Format
(PA, Vol. 4, Exh. 16, at p. 802.)

## Common Citation Issues

### Missing Pincites
Every citation that supports a specific proposition needs a page
reference. Bare volume cites are only acceptable for the general
holding of a case.

**Needs pincite:** A legal proposition followed by a case cite
without a specific page.
**Acceptable without pincite:** "The Supreme Court addressed this
issue in [Case]" when referring to the case generally.

### Orphaned Short Forms
A short-form citation (Party, supra, ...) must appear AFTER the
full citation has been given. If a short form appears first, the
reader can't find the full cite.

### Inconsistent Party Names
The short form must use the same party name as the full cite.
If the full cite says "Automotriz Del Golfo De California v.
Resnick," the short form should use "Automotriz" consistently,
not switch between "Automotriz" and "Del Golfo."

### Explanatory Parentheticals
Avoid naked cites — include a brief parenthetical explaining
the relevance of the authority. Not required for every citation,
but expected for authorities that are doing meaningful work in
the argument (as opposed to well-known standards or foundational
rules).

Example:
(Sonora Diamond Corp. v. Superior Court (2000) 83 Cal.App.4th 523,
538-539 [alter ego targets use of corporate form "to perpetrate a
fraud or injustice"].)
